## Project Proposal

**Team Members:** Teodor, Zachary, Chingis

**Idea Name:** Student Store-front

### 1. Product Hypothesis

* **Users:** Students and the campus community.
* **Problem:** Decentralized and unorganized sales platforms
* **Current Behavior:** Users rely on WhatsApp groups and waste time digging through old messages to see what's still for sale. Frequently, items are already sold but remain in the chat.
* **Changed Behavior:** Eliminates wasted time by providing a central platform for campus listings, with easy posting and status updates.

### 2. Assumptions (Initial Set)

* Users want to move away from a WhatsApp-based system to a centralized app.
* Users are willing to trade "reach" for "organization."
* Users prioritize security and trust (knowing the seller is a real student)
* Students who only want to view listings can access the platform via a QR code/URL without downloading an app or creating an account.
* Students who want to post items need to download the app to create and manage their listings.

### 3. Core Features (MVP)

* Campus Listings Board: Students can quickly browse categorized listings and see what is currently available.
* WhatsApp Bot Bridge: Students can forward old posts or create new posts through WhatsApp, which are instantly added to the app via the bot.
* Verified Student Community: Unlike open WhatsApp groups, our platform requires sellers to verify their identity via a @constructor.university email.
* Status Updates: Students can mark items as "For Sale" or "Sold" via the WhatsApp bot.
* Magic Link Access: Sellers manage their dashboard via a secure, passwordless link sent directly to their WhatsApp.

### 4. Risk Mitigation

* WhatsApp Transition: Users forward posts to the bot, ensuring legal compliance while populating the platform.
* Onboarding Friction: The app is available as a Progressive Web App, so students can access it via a QR code without downloading anything.
* Market Validation: Collect feedback from students using the MVP to refine features and confirm demand before adding new functionality.
